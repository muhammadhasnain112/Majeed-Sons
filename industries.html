Industries & Applications

Rubber and foam components specified wherever sealing, cushioning, insulation or vibration control is required.

Automotive

NVH pads, weatherseals and gaskets.

Construction

Joints, membranes and safety flooring.

PE expansion joints, rubber sheets and mats for commercial buildings and infrastructure.

Electronics

Silicone pads and enclosure gaskets.

Soft-touch protection, thermal interface pads and sealing for cabinets and devices.

Packaging

EVA and PE protective inserts.

Custom foam cavities that protect instruments, spare parts and export goods in transit.

HVAC

Insulation and machine isolation.

Closed-cell EPDM foam, duct gaskets and anti-vibration pads for air-handling plant.

Sports & Fitness

Mats, EVA foam and shock pads.

Gym flooring, playground underlay and sports-goods foam components.

Furniture

Sheets, gaskets and custom parts.
