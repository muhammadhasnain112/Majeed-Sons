About Us

A manufacturing and trading partner built around specification, reliability and long-term industrial supply.

Who We Are

Your Trusted Partner in Rubber & Foam Solutions

EVA Rubber Foam provides rubber, foam and customized industrial products to businesses that cannot afford inconsistent quality. We combine conversion capability with a practical trading network so buyers get the grade, thickness and finish their application actually needs.

From Karachi we serve OEM, construction, HVAC, packaging and general industrial customers across Pakistan and export markets. The company name, contact details and logo are structured so they can be replaced when you brand the site as your own.

How We Work

A Straightforward Supply Approach

01. Understand the duty

We start with temperature, media, load and environment so the compound is chosen for the application, not only the price list.

02. Specify the product

Thickness, density, colour, tolerance and fabrication method are confirmed before production or dispatch.

03. Deliver with evidence

Batches are inspected, packed for industrial handling and supported with documentation when required.
