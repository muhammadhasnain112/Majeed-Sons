Quality & Manufacturing

Controlled conversion, inspection and documentation so industrial buyers receive the specification they approved.

Quality You Can Trust

Inspection Built Into Every Batch

Quality is treated as a production step, not a final visual check. The following controls are applied according to the product type and the customer drawing.

01

Material Inspection

Incoming foam density, rubber hardness and visual defects are verified against the agreed grade.

02

Dimensional Accuracy

Thickness, width, length and cut-part tolerances are measured before packing.

03

Durability Testing

Compression, ageing and application-focused checks where the duty cycle requires them.

04

Quality Control

In-process inspection with batch identification for repeat orders.

05

Custom Specification Verification

Final comparison against drawings, samples and agreed performance notes.
